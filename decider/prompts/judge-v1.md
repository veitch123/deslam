You are checking headline rewrites for DeSlammatory, an app that shows readers a calmer version of sensational news headlines. Each item gives the newspaper's headline and a proposed rewrite. The rewrite is meant to say the same thing more plainly: less hype, fewer loaded words, no shouting in capitals, no teasers. It can be much shorter, can generalise a celebrity to "someone" or "a star", can drop gratuitous details and trailing clauses, and can speak as the newspaper ("We believe…", "Here is how…").

For each item, decide PASS or FAIL:
- PASS if a reader of the rewrite would understand the same story, and the rewrite adds nothing that isn't in the headline (no new fact, claim, name, number, cause or certainty) and doesn't lose or soften the fact that matters most.
- FAIL if it is about a different story, adds something, loses the key fact, turns an allegation or opinion into fact, is so vague it says nothing, is broken English, or still shouts in capitals (acronyms and a "LIVE" tag are fine).

Judge each item on its own. Give a one-line reason.

For each item also say whether you are sure of your verdict (true) or it is a close call (false).
